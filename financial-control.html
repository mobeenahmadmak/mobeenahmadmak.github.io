Financial control framework

A connected cycle from transaction review through financial reporting and external audit support.

01Close & ReconcileCoordinate period-end activities, review ledger balances and complete key reconciliations.
02Report under IFRSPrepare financial statements and management reporting from reviewed accounting records.
03Comply & FileManage VAT reporting and ZATCA-related compliance and e-invoicing requirements.
04Audit & ImproveCoordinate audit schedules, respond to information requests and follow up on observations.

Key areas of responsibility

The finance activities covered across reporting periods and compliance cycles.

01 · Financial reporting

IFRS reporting & financial statements

Maintaining the integrity and consistency of financial information for management and statutory reporting.

  • Preparation and review of IFRS-based financial statements.
  • General ledger oversight and balance sheet reconciliations.
  • Review of accounting treatments, accruals and period-end adjustments.
  • Coordination of supporting schedules and financial reporting deliverables.
02 · Period-end discipline

Monthly, quarterly & annual closing

Coordinating recurring close activities to support timely and accurate financial reporting.

  • Planning and coordinating period-end close activities.
  • Reviewing ledger balances, journals and reconciliations.
  • Coordinating AP, AR, payroll, fixed asset and bank reconciliation inputs.
  • Preparing closing reports and resolving identified discrepancies.
03 · Regulatory compliance

VAT, ZATCA & e-invoicing

Supporting tax compliance through accurate accounting records, reporting and system processes.

  • VAT return preparation, review and filing coordination.
  • ZATCA compliance and e-invoicing implementation support.
  • Reconciliation of VAT balances with accounting records.
  • Follow-up on tax reporting requirements and related documentation.
04 · Assurance & governance

Audit coordination & internal controls

Organizing audit support and strengthening the quality of financial control documentation.

  • Coordinating external audit requests, schedules and supporting documents.
  • Preparing account reconciliations and audit working papers.
  • Following up on audit queries and observations with relevant teams.
  • Supporting control improvements across finance processes.

Process improvement priorities

Practical improvements that support a more controlled and repeatable finance function.

Standardized close procedures

Clear ownership, recurring checklists and reconciliations to bring consistency to monthly, quarterly and annual closing.

Reconciliation discipline

Structured review of balance sheet accounts, VAT balances, bank activity and supporting schedules.

Compliance-ready records

Organized tax records, e-invoicing processes and supporting documentation to facilitate reporting and review.

Audit readiness

Well-maintained schedules, traceable supporting documents and coordinated responses to audit requests.
